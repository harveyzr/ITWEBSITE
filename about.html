About TechCPA Solutions

Your trusted technology partners who understand the unique needs of CPA firms and accounting professionals

Our Story

Founded in 2015, TechCPA Solutions was born from a simple observation: CPA firms needed technology partners who truly understood their business. Our founder, a former IT director at a major accounting firm, recognized that generic IT support wasn't enough for the unique challenges faced by tax professionals.

What started as a small consulting practice has grown into a comprehensive IT services company serving over 500 CPA firms across the United States. We've maintained our focus on specialization, ensuring that every member of our team understands the intricacies of tax preparation, compliance requirements, and the seasonal demands of accounting practices.

Today, we're proud to be the go-to technology partner for CPA firms of all sizes, from solo practitioners to large regional firms. Our success is measured not just in uptime and response times, but in the peace of mind we provide to accounting professionals who trust us with their most critical technology needs.
