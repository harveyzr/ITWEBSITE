Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly can you respond to support requests?

We guarantee a 15-minute response time for critical issues during business hours, and within 2 hours for general inquiries. During tax season, we provide 24/7 support with even faster response times.

Do you provide on-site support?

Yes, we offer on-site support for our Enterprise clients and can arrange on-site visits for complex installations or urgent issues. Most problems can be resolved remotely for faster resolution.

What makes your Lacerte support different?

Our team consists of certified Lacerte specialists who understand tax preparation workflows. We don't just fix technical issues – we help optimize your processes for maximum efficiency.

Can you help with data migration?

Absolutely! We specialize in migrating data between tax software versions, moving to cloud solutions, and ensuring your historical data remains accessible and secure.

What security measures do you implement?

We follow IRS Publication 4557 guidelines and implement multi-factor authentication, encryption, regular security audits, and compliance reporting to protect your sensitive client data.

Do you offer training for our staff?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training on software usage, security best practices, and workflow optimization. Training can be conducted on-site or remotely based on your preference.
